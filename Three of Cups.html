Three of Cups (3oC): Paid Ads Strategy for a Groundbreaking Tech Startup

The goal was to help Three of Cups (3oC) successfully launch its first paid advertising campaign—starting from scratch with no prior marketing data, ad accounts, or digital tracking setup. To achieve this, I created a full-funnel strategy that moved users from awareness to conversion, identified and segmented the target audience, and selected key platforms like Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn. I also developed a detailed $10,000 budget plan, defined clear performance metrics, and recommended tools for A/B testing, tracking, and optimization. The result was a scalable, data-driven marketing roadmap designed to help 3oC build trust, drive sign-ups, and grow as a leading platform in the digital identity verification space.
